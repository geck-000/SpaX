billing cost. slab_vof = t(1-b) so each cell realises its intended pair, which
makes t constant along a row by construction and a variation in n there a
variation in b alone. One seed per condition: seed scatter has run 0.004 to

the replicate was not earning its solve time. t=0.14 b=0.10 carries two seeds
and they agree.
